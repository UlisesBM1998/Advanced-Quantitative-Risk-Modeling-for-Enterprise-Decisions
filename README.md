🎯 Five end-to-end risk analytics projects in Python, each built around a real business decision and quantified empirical conclusions. Together they show how statistics and actuarial methods turn data into decisions. Tools: pandas, scikit-learn, statsmodels, SciPy, pyspark, SQL.

🎯 Credit Risk & Decision Optimization
Logistic regression for probability of default (AUC 0.955), Expected Credit Loss (PD × LGD × EAD), approval-threshold optimization and stress testing. Expected losses rise 37% in a downturn and 72% in a severe scenario.

🎯 Market Risk (VaR)
Historical, parametric and Monte Carlo VaR on a 12-asset portfolio, with Expected Shortfall, crisis-window stress tests and a Kupiec backtest. The COVID window pushed 99% VaR 110% above the full-period figure.

🎯 Healthcare Insurance Fraud
Leakage-controlled scoring model validated out of time (ROC-AUC 0.967), Bühlmann-Straub credibility to rank providers fairly, and a frequency-severity Monte Carlo for annual fraud loss. Reviewing the top 10% of claims captures 71% of fraud claims and 84% of fraud dollars.

🎯 Operational Risk Analysis in Industrial Reliability
Failure-probability model with PCA to discard noisy sensors, Kaplan-Meier and Weibull survival analysis, and Random Forest and XGBoost risk ranking. Machines are most exposed right after startup or maintenance (Weibull shape 0.79, median 64 hours between failures). Reducing costs by approximately 57% (from $405,000 to $175,500).
