Five end-to-end risk analytics projects in Python, each built around a real business situations and quantified empirical conclusions. Together they show how statistics and actuarial methods turn data into decisions. Tools: pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, SciPy, lifelines, statsmodels, XGBoost, SQLAlchemy, PySpark.

🎯 **Credit Risk & Decision Optimization:** Default-prediction pipeline with logistic regression, Expected Credit Loss (PD × LGD × EAD), approval-threshold optimization, and scenario stress testing. The model reaches 0.955 AUC with 87% recall on defaults, and a 3-feature version (interest rate, debt-to-income, income) still holds 0.902. Expected loss on $30.6M of exposure is $6.15M (20.1%) in the base case and rises 72% to $10.6M under a severe downturn.

🎯 **Customer Lifetime Value & Pension Longevity Risk:** Survival model with Kaplan-Meier, Cox Proportional Hazards, Weibull AFT extrapolation, and Monte Carlo mortality simulations across 5,024 pensioners. Smoking and chronic conditions drive mortality while pension type does not (log-rank p = 0.75), and modeled liabilities of $171M sit well below $348M in booked reserves, with accrued assets covering 75.8% of those reserves. A +20% longevity shock lifts liabilities only 7.9%, with 95% VaR at $176.6M and 99% TVaR at $184.4M.

🎯 **Healthcare Insurance Fraud Analysis:** Risk and Financial Impact Logistic scoring model (ROC-AUC 0.967 on a time-based holdout), Bühlmann-Straub credibility for provider risk, and a frequency-severity Monte Carlo for annual losses. Reviewing the top 5% of claims ranked by expected value finds fraud at a 72.7% hit rate versus 7.1% for random review, netting $69.6K under SIU capacity limits. Annual fraud loss averages $209K and reaches $258.5K at 99% expected shortfall, with 10 of 300 providers above the 99.8% funnel limit.

🎯 **Market Risk Value at Risk (VaR):** Multi-method VaR and Expected Shortfall framework for a 12-asset portfolio using Historical Simulation, Variance-Covariance, and Monte Carlo with Cholesky-correlated scenarios, validated with stress windows and a Kupiec backtest. The three methods put 1-day 99% VaR between $2.0K and $2.2K, but the COVID crash window lifts it to $4.6K (+110%), and 99% Expected Shortfall is $2.85K. The rolling 99% VaR had 12 breaches (1.52% vs 1% expected) and passed Kupiec (p = 0.17), with a 10-day VaR of $6.9K and an illustrative ×3 capital buffer of $20.8K.

🎯 **Operational Risk Analysis in Industrial Reliability:** Predictive maintenance framework for 7 IoT-monitored machines using logistic risk scoring, Kaplan-Meier and Weibull survival analysis, Random Forest and XGBoost classifiers (AUC 0.87 and 0.81). Time-based features (days running, days since inspection) drive failures more than vibration, noise, RPM or humidity, and a Weibull shape of 0.79 points to early-life failures. At a 0.35 alert threshold the model catches 6 of 9 test failures, cutting estimated maintenance cost from $405K to $175.5K (−57%).
